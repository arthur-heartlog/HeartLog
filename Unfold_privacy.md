# Privacy Policy | 隐私政策

**Effective Date | 生效日期: May 27, 2026**

---

### [English Version]

Your privacy is our absolute priority, especially when it concerns your personal financial and subscription data. This Privacy Policy describes how **Unfold** (the "App") handles your information.

#### 1. Data Collection and Usage
**Unfold does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal, financial, or subscription data on external servers.** 
All information processed by the App, including but not limited to:
- Subscription service names, pricing, and billing dates.
- Monthly/yearly budget settings and budget usage history.
- "Subscription Burn Rate" calculations and expense analytics.
- Personal profile information (Nickname and Avatar).
- App preferences and localization settings.

**Remains exclusively on your device.** We have zero access to your data, and we do not sell or share it with any third parties.

#### 2. Data Storage
The App uses Apple's native **SwiftData** framework to store your information locally on your iPhone or iPad. This data is protected by iOS's built-in sandbox security features and hardware-level encryption. Your custom avatar images are stored securely in the App's private Documents directory.

#### 3. Third-Party Services
Unfold does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. There is no cloud synchronization or external API communication for your private financial records.

#### 4. User Control and Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally, you have full control:
- You can clear or reset all subscriptions and budgets within the App's settings menu.
- To delete all data associated with Unfold, simply uninstall the App. Once uninstalled, all local data is permanently wiped and cannot be recovered.

#### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
**HeartLog@icloud.com**

### [中文版]

您的隐私是我们的核心关切，尤其涉及您的个人财务与订阅数据。本隐私政策描述了 **Unfold**（下称“本应用”）如何处理您的信息。

#### 1. 数据收集与使用
**Unfold 不会在外部服务器上收集、传输或存储您的任何个人、财务或订阅数据。**
您在应用中生成的所有信息（包括但不限于）：
- 订阅的服务名称、价格、扣款周期及账单日。
- 月度/年度预算设置及预算使用历史。
- “订阅消耗率”计算结果及支出分析报表。
- 个人资料信息（昵称与头像）。
- 应用偏好与本地化设置。

**所有数据仅存储在您的设备本地。** 我们无法读取您的任何数据，也不会将您的个人财务隐私出售或分享给任何第三方。

#### 2. 数据存储
本应用使用苹果原生的 **SwiftData** 框架将您的信息存储在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 本地。这些数据由 iOS 内置的沙盒安全功能和硬件级加密技术提供保护。您的个人头像文件安全地存储在应用的私有文档目录中。

#### 3. 第三方服务
Unfold 不使用任何第三方分析、追踪或广告 SDK。您的私人财务与账单数据不存在任何形式的云端同步或隐性的外部 API 通信。

#### 4. 用户控制与数据删除
由于所有数据均存储在本地，您对其拥有完全控制权：
- 您可以在应用内的设置菜单中随时修改、重置或一键清除所有订阅及预算数据。
- 如需删除与 Unfold 相关的所有数据，只需从设备中卸载应用即可。卸载后，所有本地数据将被永久清除且无法恢复。

#### 5. 联系我们
如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或疑虑，请通过以下方式联系我们：
**HeartLog@icloud.com**
