# FurBaby Diary - Smart Pet Care & Health Tracker | 毛孩子日记 - 科学智能养宠与健康守护

**FurBaby Diary** is a warm, intelligent, and private pet care companion designed for dedicated pet parents. Featuring dynamic real-time food and water consumption tracking, intelligent adaptive feeding algorithms, comprehensive medical and expense management, and seamless iCloud sync, it empowers pet lovers to provide their furry companions with meticulous, scientific, and heartwarming care.

**毛孩子日记 (FurBaby Diary)** 是一款温暖、智能且高度注重隐私的科学养宠伴侣应用。通过动态实时水粮消耗估算、独创的自适应喂养学习算法、全维度的医疗与账单管理，以及无缝的 iCloud 云端同步，让每位铲屎官都能以科学、细致且温暖的方式记录与陪伴毛孩子的每一天成长。

---

## 🌟 Key Features | 核心功能

### [English]
- **Dynamic Real-Time Food & Water Tracking**: Intelligently simulates and visualizes the depletion rate of water bowls and food dispensers based on weight, age, neutered status, and ambient seasonal temperatures.
- **Adaptive Feeding & Watering Algorithm**: Automatically fine-tunes metabolic coefficients (RER/DER) when you calibrate bowl levels, learning your pets' unique drinking and eating habits over time.
- **Comprehensive Medical & Event Timeline**: Effortlessly manage vaccinations, internal/external deworming, clinical visits, medical prescriptions, and custom care reminders with smart notification scheduling.
- **Visualized Pet Expense Analytics**: Category-based expense tracking (Food, Medical, Grooming, Toys, Boarding) equipped with multi-dimensional monthly trend charts and budget alerts.
- **Food Safety Red-Black List**: Built-in vet-reviewed encyclopedia detailing foods that are safe, caution-advised, or strictly toxic to dogs and cats, along with emergency first-aid recommendations.
- **Privacy First & iCloud Cloud Sync**: Powered by Apple's native CoreData & CloudKit. Your sensitive pet data belongs exclusively to you—safely synchronized across your personal devices with zero third-party data tracking.

### [中文]
- **动态实时水粮流失模拟**：基于体重、年龄阶段、绝育状态及季节气温等科学参数，实时动态模拟饮水盆与粮碗的水位及余粮下降速度，缺水缺粮一目了然。
- **自适应学习校准算法**：独创自适应喂养学习模型。当您在主页手动校准碗盆比例时，系统会自动校正静息与活动代谢系数（RER/DER），越来越懂毛孩子的真实食量。
- **全方位健康档案与日程提醒**：轻松管理疫苗接种、体内外驱虫、就医处方单、体型体重成长足迹，并配合精准的多级本地通知，不错过任何重要日程。
- **多维度养宠账单与花销分析**：专为养宠家庭设计的记账工具（主粮零食、医疗保健、美容洗护、寄养出行等），生成清晰的月度开销走势图表与预算看板。
- **宠物食物红黑榜与急救指引**：内置经过专业考证的宠物食品安全科普百科，一键检索可食、慎食与严禁食用的食物清单，提供及时的急救应对措施。
- **100% 隐私安全与 iCloud 自动同步**：基于 Apple 原生 CoreData 与 CloudKit 技术构建。所有档案、照片与账单数据严格存储于设备本地与您的私有 iCloud，零第三方服务器追踪，安全防丢。

## 🔒 Privacy Policy | 隐私政策

### [English]
- **Zero Third-Party Tracking**: We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising platforms.
- **Local & iCloud Storage Only**: All your data—including pet profiles, logs, medical records, expenses, and photos—is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your personal private iCloud database managed directly by Apple.
- **No Data Collection**: We do not collect, view, sell, or share your personal data, pet records, or financial information.
- **In-App Purchases & Subscriptions**: All transactions and subscriptions are handled securely by Apple StoreKit. We do not process or store any of your payment or credit card details.

### [中文]
- **零第三方追踪**：我们不集成任何第三方数据分析、跟踪 SDK 或广告推送平台。
- **仅本地与私有 iCloud 存储**：您录入的所有数据（包括宠物档案、日常日记、医疗单据、记账记录及照片）均仅保存在您的本地设备中；若开通同步，数据将直接存入 Apple 托管的个人私有 iCloud 容器中。
- **绝不收集用户数据**：我们不收集、不访问、不出售、不共享您的任何个人数据、宠物档案及消费账单。
- **应用内购买与订阅**：所有内购和会员订阅均通过 Apple 官方 StoreKit 2 进行安全处理，应用不会收集或接触您的任何银行卡或支付信息。
