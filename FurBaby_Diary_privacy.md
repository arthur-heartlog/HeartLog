# Privacy Policy | 隐私政策

**Effective Date | 生效日期: September 19, 2026**

---

### [English Version]

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us, especially when it concerns your cherished pet records and personal expense data. This Privacy Policy describes how **FurBaby Diary** (the "App") handles your information.

#### 1. Data Collection and Usage
**FurBaby Diary does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or pet data on external third-party servers.**
All information processed by the App, including but not limited to:
- Pet profiles (names, avatars, species, breed, gender, birthday, neutered status).
- Dynamic water and food consumption calculations and custom calibrations.
- Health timelines (vaccinations, internal/external deworming, clinical visits, medical prescriptions, and notes).
- Growth footprint records (weight history, body size, photos, and memorial notes).
- Expense records, categories, monthly budgets, and inventory stock tracking.
- App preferences and localization settings.

**Remains strictly under your personal control.** We have zero access to your data, and we never sell, track, or share it with any third parties.

#### 2. Data Storage & iCloud Synchronization
- **Local Storage**: The App uses Apple's native **CoreData** framework to store your information locally on your device. This data is protected by iOS's built-in sandbox security features and hardware-level encryption. All pet avatars, medical receipts, and photos are stored securely in the App's private local sandboxed directory.
- **iCloud Sync**: When you subscribe to Pro and enable iCloud sync, your data is synced across your own Apple devices exclusively through Apple's native **CloudKit** infrastructure into your personal private iCloud database. The developer has no access, viewing, or editing rights to your private iCloud container.

#### 3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
All transactions, subscriptions (Monthly, Annual), and non-consumable Lifetime purchases are handled securely and directly through Apple's official **StoreKit 2** framework. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, Apple Pay, or any payment information.

#### 4. Third-Party Services & Tracking
FurBaby Diary does not integrate any third-party analytics, diagnostic tracking SDKs, or advertising platforms. There are no advertising identifiers (IDFA) tracked or third-party cookies utilized.

#### 5. User Control and Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally and in your private iCloud, you have full control over your data:
- You can freely edit, archive, or delete individual pet archives, medical records, and bills inside the App.
- To completely delete all data associated with FurBaby Diary, simply uninstall the App from your device and clear the App's storage from your iCloud Settings. Once deleted, all local data is permanently wiped.

#### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us:  
**HeartLog@icloud.com**

### [中文版]

您的隐私对我们至关重要，特别是涉及您珍贵的宠物档案与日常记账数据。本隐私政策描述了 **FurBaby Diary (毛孩子日记)**（下称“本应用”）如何处理您的信息。

#### 1. 数据收集与使用
**毛孩子日记不会在任何外部第三方服务器上收集、传输或存储您的任何个人或宠物数据。**  
您在应用中生成的所有信息（包括但不限于）：
- 宠物档案资料（昵称、头像、种类、品种、性别、生日、绝育状态等）。
- 动态饮水与食量模拟计算参数及自适应校准记录。
- 健康与医疗日程（疫苗接种、体内外驱虫、就医处方单、病历照片及日程备忘）。
- 成长足迹（体重历史、体型记录、照片以及纪念馆归档记录）。
- 养宠开销记账记录、分类、月度预算及日常物资库存数据。
- 应用偏好、个性化主题与语言设置。

**所有数据完全由您个人掌控。** 我们无法读取您的任何数据，绝不追踪您的日常活动，也绝不会将您的个人隐私出售或共享给任何第三方。

#### 2. 数据存储与 iCloud 云端同步
- **本地存储**：本应用使用苹果原生的 **CoreData** 框架将您的信息存储在您的设备本地。数据由 iOS 内置的沙盒安全机制和芯片级硬件加密提供保护。您拍摄或上传的宠物照片、病历单据均存储在应用的私有本地沙盒目录中。
- **iCloud 云同步**：当您订阅 Pro 会员并开启云同步时，数据将通过 Apple 原生的 **CloudKit** 框架直接同步至您个人 Apple ID 绑定的私有 iCloud 容器中。开发者无权访问、查看或修改您的任何私有 iCloud 云端数据。

#### 3. 应用内购买与会员订阅
本应用内所有的 Pro 会员订阅（月度、年度）及终身买断交易，均由 Apple 官方 **StoreKit 2** 系统底层直接完成。我们不会收集、处理或接触您的银行卡号、Apple Pay 或任何支付账单信息。

#### 4. 第三方服务与追踪
毛孩子日记不集成任何第三方数据分析 SDK、错误追踪 SDK 或商业广告 SDK。我们不获取 IDFA（广告标识符），也不进行任何跨应用的行为追踪。

#### 5. 用户控制与数据删除
由于所有数据均保存在您的本地设备及您的私有 iCloud 中，您拥有完全的数据控制权：
- 您可以随时在应用内添加、编辑、归档或彻底删除单只宠物、医疗日志及账单记录。
- 如需彻底清除与毛孩子日记相关的全部数据，只需直接从设备上卸载本应用，并在 iOS「设置 > Apple ID > iCloud > 管理账户储存空间」中删除该应用的云端备份即可。卸载后，本地数据将被永久销毁。

#### 6. 联系我们
如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或反馈，欢迎通过以下方式联系我们：  
**HeartLog@icloud.com**
