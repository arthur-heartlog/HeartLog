# Unfold - A Premium Subscription Tracker | Unfold - 财务透明化订阅管理专家

**Unfold** is a minimalist and elegant iOS application designed to bring ultimate transparency to your digital subscriptions. By combining high-end modern design with the unique "Subscription Burn Rate" visualization and dynamic budget feedback, it empowers you to uncover hidden recurring costs and take back control of your financial destiny.

**Unfold (展开)** 是一款极简且优雅的 iOS 应用，专为实现财务透明度与优化订阅成本而生。通过将高级感现代设计与独创的“订阅消耗率”可视化以及动态预算光圈相结合，它帮助您彻底清扫暗处的自动扣费，重新掌握日常财务的主动权。

---

## 🌟 Key Features | 核心功能

### [English]
- **Dynamic Pulse Dashboard**: Monitor your monthly budget health instantly through the color-shifting "Financial Pulse" ring (Green for safe, Orange for caution, Red for over-budget).
- **Subscription Burn Rate**: Translate monthly bills into real-time visual metrics (cost per hour or minute) to truly understand the long-term impact of micro-expenses.
- **Smart Renewal Alerts**: Proactive multi-stage notifications 24 to 48 hours before any renewal, eliminating unwanted "silent auto-renewals".
- **Deep Expense Analytics**: Visualized spending habits powered by sleek iOS charts showing monthly trends and category breakdowns (Video, Music, SaaS, Storage).
- **100% Privacy & Security**: Powered by Apple's modern SwiftData. All financial records are stored strictly on your device. Zero cloud tracking, completely secure.

### [中文]
- **动态脉动仪表盘**：主屏幕配备高灵动的“Financial Pulse”环形预算光圈，根据当月预算健康度动态变换颜色（绿色安全、橙色预警、红色超支），资产状况一目了然。
- **订阅消耗率**：独创将包月/包年费用转化为“每小时 / 每分钟支出”的视觉指标，以微观视角揭开长线持续性开支的真实面目。
- **智能扣费预警**：在账单到期前 24 至 48 小时发送多级智能推送提醒，拒绝任何“静默自动续期”套路，留出充足的决策时间。
- **深度消费分析**：结合 iOS 现代图表，生成精美的趋势曲线与分类分布图（视频、音乐、办公软件、云存储），精准找出功能重叠或不必要的支出。
- **绝对隐私安全**：基于 Apple 现代 SwiftData 数据持久化技术，数据完全离线存储在设备本地，不收集、不上传任何账单数据，支持 Apple ID 登录。
